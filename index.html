Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Block Puzzle 8×8 (package com.demiurgus.blockpuzzle, “the app”), published by Demiurgus (“we”, “us”), handles information. We built the app to collect as little as possible.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information on our own servers. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no analytics of our own, and we do not operate any server that receives your data.

The following is stored only on your device so the game works and can be resumed:

  • your best score and the current game (board, pieces, score);
  • settings (sound, vibration);
  • the number of undos you purchased and a list of processed purchase IDs (to avoid crediting the same purchase twice).

This data never leaves your device through us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads (only when you choose to watch one for an extra undo or to continue a game). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process, on its own behalf:

  • device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and ad interaction data.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form before personalised ads are used, and you can choose non-personalised ads. On iOS the app asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, the IDFA is not shared. You can also reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

Undo packs are sold through Google Play. Payments and billing details are handled entirely by Google under its privacy policy. We never see or store your payment information; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so the undos can be added in the app.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are requested with settings appropriate to its content rating.

6. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s own retention policies.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage, and you can control ad personalisation in the in-app consent form (where shown) and in your device settings. For data processed by Google, see Google’s privacy tools.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Politika privatnosti

Važi od: 15. novembra 2026.

Ova politika objašnjava kako mobilna igra Block Puzzle 8×8 (paket com.demiurgus.blockpuzzle, „aplikacija“), izdavača Demiurgus („mi“), postupa sa podacima. Aplikacija je napravljena tako da prikuplja što manje.

1. Koje podatke prikupljamo

Ne prikupljamo, ne čuvamo i ne delimo lične podatke na svojim serverima. Aplikacija nema naloge, prijavu, čet ni sopstvenu analitiku, i nemamo server koji prima tvoje podatke.

Sledeće se čuva samo na tvom uređaju, da bi igra radila i mogla da se nastavi:

  • najbolji rezultat i trenutna partija (tabla, oblici, poeni);
  • podešavanja (zvuk, vibracija);
  • broj kupljenih vraćanja i spisak obrađenih kupovina (da se ista kupovina ne bi uračunala dva puta).

Ovi podaci ne napuštaju uređaj preko nas. Brišu se kada deinstaliraš aplikaciju ili obrišeš njene podatke.

2. Reklame (Google AdMob)

Aplikacija prikazuje opcione nagradne video reklame (samo kad sam izabereš da gledaš reklamu za dodatno vraćanje ili nastavak igre). Reklame obezbeđuje Google AdMob. Da bi prikazao, merio i ograničio reklame i sprečio prevare, Google može u svoje ime da prikuplja i obrađuje:

  • reklamni identifikator uređaja (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP adresu i približnu lokaciju određenu na osnovu nje;
  • podatke o uređaju i aplikaciji (model, verzija sistema, jezik, verzija aplikacije) i podatke o interakciji sa reklamama.

Ako si u Evropskom ekonomskom prostoru, Ujedinjenom Kraljevstvu ili Švajcarskoj, aplikacija pre personalizovanih reklama prikazuje Google-ov obrazac za saglasnost, i možeš izabrati nepersonalizovane reklame. Na iOS-u aplikacija traži dozvolu za praćenje (App Tracking Transparency); ako je odbiješ, IDFA se ne deli. Reklamni ID možeš resetovati ili isključiti u podešavanjima uređaja.

Više: Kako Google koristi podatke iz aplikacija koje koriste njegove usluge · Google politika privatnosti.

3. Kupovine u aplikaciji

Paketi vraćanja se prodaju preko Google Play prodavnice. Plaćanje i podatke za naplatu u potpunosti obrađuje Google prema svojoj politici privatnosti. Mi nikada ne vidimo niti čuvamo tvoje podatke o plaćanju; dobijamo samo potvrdu da je kupovina završena, da bi se vraćanja dodala u igri.

Podaci igre ostaju na tvom uređaju. Podatke koje prikuplja Google čuva se prema pravilima Google-a.
